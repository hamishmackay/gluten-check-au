Allergen information discrepancy

What to do: Do not rely on this product as gluten-free until clarified. Keep the packaging, best-before/batch details and a copy of the online listing. Ask the manufacturer or retailer whether the warning applies to your exact pack. If a mandatory allergen may be undeclared, contact your state food authority.

A differing voluntary “may contain” warning alone does not prove a breach of Australian labelling law.
